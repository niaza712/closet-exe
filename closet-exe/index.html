03 / Research question

“When our identities are filtered through categories, what gets left out?”

This prototype isn't trying to improve recommendation accuracy. It asks participants to compare a machine-readable label with a story that resists classification.

04 / Participate

Micro-interview protocol

Try the archive with three people. Ask them to bring an item they would never throw away. Record what a classifier would say, what they say, and where the accounts diverge.
